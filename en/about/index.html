About

Emma Cochet is an interior architect based in Paris. She founded Studio Emma Cochet after graduating from the Royal College of Art in London. Her background has fostered a particular sensitivity to enhancing and respecting what already exists, in a subtle dialogue between old and contemporary.

This approach extends into her furniture collection, where she gives antique pieces a new life through bold intervention.
